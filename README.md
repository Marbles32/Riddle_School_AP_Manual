# Riddle_School_AP_Manual
An Archipelago Manual created for the Riddle School series by Marbles32.
(Please note that I do not support the actions of the Riddle School creator, Jonochrome. This is created from the love I have for Riddle School as a series.)

Phil Eggtree wants to escape school, but he seems to have completely forgotten how to use any items! Seems like he'll need some help from the multiworld...
This manual covers the games: Riddle School 1-5, and Riddle Transfer 1 + 2.
To play the Riddle School series for yourself, I recommend using BlueMaxima's Flashpoint Archive.
The goal is to complete all 7 games in the series.
Every room and item pickup is a location. You may freely pick up items so long as you have access to them, however you will need to get an item from the multiworld in order to actually use it.
For example, in Riddle School 2, you can pick up the four quarters at the beginning regardless of your checks. However, you may not trade them in for the whistle until you get the "Money" check.
The exception to this is Riddle School 5's dream segments. No items are needed to complete these. However, you will need the "Escape Pods" item in order to progress past them.

Currently, the manual is not completed. I have currently finished Riddle School 1, 2, and 4, and hope to work on the remaining four when I can.
(However, I have completely mapped out the logic for every game, so hopefully they shouldn't take too long...)
